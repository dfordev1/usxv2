    An-Nās shows the real reason milestones exist. Look at
    ayah 3 (إِلَـٰهِ النَّاسِ) — the KFGQPC V2 page
    layout breaks mid-ayah, between line 12 and line 13, right in
    the middle of the word pair. A tree-nested format (page → ayah → word)
    cannot represent that without either splitting the ayah element in two or
    duplicating the word. QUSX's flat word stream with independent sid/eid
    pins handles it without either — page and ayah are just two boundary
    axes crossing the same stream, exactly as USX's own milestone design
    intends. Click any word to see its real id, morphology, and
    which axes are open at that point.
